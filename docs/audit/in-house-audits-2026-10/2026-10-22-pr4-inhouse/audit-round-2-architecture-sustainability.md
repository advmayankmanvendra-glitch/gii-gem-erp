## Verdict

**Rating: Sustainable with conditions.** The PR improves the codebase: invoice GST is computed on the server, company scoping is tighter, every IPC channel needs a session, upgraded databases get repair migration 036 with a backup first, alert dates use the local date, and the suite grew from 46 to 73 tests. It meets AGENTS.md: no business logic in screens, no IPC, preload or route-key renames. The structural debt from 10-17 is **unchanged** unless the table says otherwise: REAL money, GST in 6+ places, a growing `init.js` hub, 74 channels with no permission check, no CSP, no CI, click-only backups and a shipped build-machine database. Two problems are new: a per-company reset is rolled back by restoring the whole database, and credit due dates are one day early in IST. Neither blocks merging PR #4 as code; several block **production go-live** (Next steps 1-4).

## What breaks first as data and users grow

1. **Disk loss or a bad restore.** There is no off-disk or scheduled backup.
2. **Paise drift** between the dashboard, receivables and GST figures as invoice volume grows (Risks 4-5).
3. **Series numbering and posting checks.** `nextSeriesNumber` scans with `LIKE` (O(n)), and posting validates before the transaction opens. Both are unsafe once a second writer appears *(inferred; unchanged)*.
4. **Permissions.** The 74 unguarded channels and the lack of per-company permissions matter once non-admin users are added.

## Prioritised next steps (before production go-live unless noted)

1. Automatic, rotated backups to a second location, with a SHA-256 manifest holding the last audit hash. Include the runtime JSON files (alerts, go-live and certificate history). **Go-live blocker.**
2. Remove the bundled `database/gii_gem_erp.db` from `extraResources`; move `playwright` and `@vitejs/plugin-react` to devDependencies. **Release blocker.**
3. Make reset and go-live rollback company-safe: warn, or refuse, when other companies changed after the snapshot. Make `rollbackReset` refuse to fall back to "any backup" (`operationalResetService.js:346`), as go-live rollback already does. **Go-live blocker for multi-company installs.**
4. Fix the date-only `toISOString` sites (`init.js:1475`, `inventoryService.js:127`), with a TZ=Asia/Kolkata test.
5. Record owner approval for migration 036 in PR #4; take a backup before *any* pending migration; add `user_version` and a refuse-newer-schema check.
6. IPC permission-matrix test; guard `certificate:generate`, `updates:install`, `health:verification-snapshot`; take the typed confirmation from the user in the GoLiveWizard.
7. Windows CI: `test:integrity` under `ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE`, then `pack:win` + `packaged-runtime-smoke.js`.
8. One GST engine (`domain/gst.js`) and one profitability/expense function; remove the renderer and `init.js` copies.
9. Integer money for invoices, payments, receivables and GST; the dashboard reads `*_minor` only.
10. Split `init.js` into a composition root; consolidate the company-scope helpers; delete `screenRegistry.resolveScreen` or adopt it; add CSP and navigation guards; refresh the graph registry.

**PR #4 merge blockers: none at code level.** Merge after the owner records approval for migration 036 (an AGENTS.md condition). Items 1-3 must be closed before any customer go-live.
