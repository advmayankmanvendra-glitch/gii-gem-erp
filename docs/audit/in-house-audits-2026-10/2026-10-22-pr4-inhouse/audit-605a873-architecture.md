## Verdict

**Rating: Sustainable with conditions. No High finding. Four Medium.**

AGENTS.md compliance:
- **App.jsx (§2):** not touched.
- **Screens (§7):** `ProfitSimulatorScreen.jsx` only resets state and clamps inputs. No accounting rule was added to a screen.
- **IPC, preload, route keys (§4):** none renamed or added. `client.js`, `preload.cjs` and `src/renderer/config` are unchanged. The removed `sessionRequired` was dead code, not an IPC name. `sessionGuarded` gained an optional fourth argument.
- **Schema (§4, §25.3):** no `CREATE TABLE`, `ALTER TABLE`, `addColumnIfMissing` or `runMigration` in the range. The only data change is the expected seeded ledger row `Purchase Price Variance`, one per company, with one `account_group_assignments` row (`SYSTEM_SEED`).
- **Error guidance (§13):** every new refusal says what failed and what to do. This covers the manifest refusal, the Tally master cap, the stock-preview cap and the 16 MiB entity budget. The entity message ("special-character codes") is plain enough.
- **Posting and profitability (§4):**
  - EXP is implemented as approved, through one shared helper.
  - CV adds the approved PPV ledger. It also values the Trade Payables part of a return at the billed receipt price. The owner was not shown that part (A2).
  - The order-closure change narrows netting to credit-note invoices. That matches what the owner list already describes under (dd), so it needs no new approval.
  - No GST calculation was changed.

The four new tests and the extended closure test pass at `605a873`: 6 of 6 subtests run with `node --test --test-name-pattern=...`. `verify-fsr-2b1.js` was not run. It needs Electron.
