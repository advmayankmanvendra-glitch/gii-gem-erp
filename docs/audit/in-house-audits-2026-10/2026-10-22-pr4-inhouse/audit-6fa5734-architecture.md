## Verdict

**Rating: Sustainable with conditions (unchanged), plus one new High finding.**

The commit keeps to AGENTS.md:
- No business logic was added to `App.jsx` or to screens.
- No IPC channel, preload method or route key was renamed.
- There is no schema change.
- New refusals are guided ("…Nothing was saved", "…Post the bill first").

It closes or narrows four items of the previous risk table: #2, #8, #9 and #11.

The main architectural problem is the bank-details policy. It is a good, pure module, but the checks were wired into four call sites in two layers. Each site judges the **raw IPC payload**, not the row that `updateRecord` actually writes. A data-entry operator can still change a supplier's account number and IFSC through the field aliases that `crudHelpers` normalises. I confirmed this against the real handlers (F1). Fix it before the security claim in the commit message is relied on.
